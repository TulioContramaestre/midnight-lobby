# Midnight Lobby: Style Guide

This is the voice and design bible for all player-facing text: dialogue, choices, narration, Chirp posts, endings. Read it before writing or editing any content. Every example line below is from the game as shipped. When this guide and the existing content disagree, the existing content wins; update this guide.

**Golden rule: never rewrite existing lines unless explicitly asked.** New content should sound like it was always there.

---

## 1. The setting

- A gaming Discord-style server called **Midnight Lobby**, one week, Monday to Sunday.
- Everything happens through online platforms: voice channels, DMs, group chats, streams, ranked queues, the Chirp feed. Show events through platform actions (muting, pinning, reacting, status changes, unfollowing, call length), not physical scenes.
- Tone: online banter and e-dating drama, PG-13. Funny first, with real moments of sweetness. Nobody is cruel; people are dry, awkward, jealous, smug or soft.

### Heat level (hard limits)

- All characters are adults. Love interests are 21 or older. The player is 18 or older.
- The most intimate things in the game are sleep calls and matching profile pictures. Flirtation and emotional closeness only. No sexual content, no body-focused comments, nothing explicit.
- Jealousy, gossip and drama are fine. Slurs, harassment and genuinely mean-spirited content are not.

### Made-up names only

Every game, show, team and platform inside the game is a parody name. Never use real game titles, streamers, brands or real people.

| Kind | Existing names |
|---|---|
| Server | Midnight Lobby |
| Social feed | Chirp |
| Games | Spikefall (tactical shooter), Arcanum Lanes (MOBA), Vanguard Protocol (hero shooter), Omniverse Clash (superhero shooter), Petal Valley (farming sim) |
| Maps | Glasshouse, Bonehaven, Ascent Ridge, Saltwind Port, Neon Rift, Kingsrow Tower, Nebula Dock, Ironvale |
| Shows/films | Starlight Requiem, Blade of the Quiet Moon, My Roommate Is a Dungeon Boss, Ghost Shift, The Hollow Below, Room Zero, Static Bloom, Do Not Answer, Paper Lanterns, The Little Lighthouse, Sprout and the Moon |
| Esports teams | Team Nightjar, Crimson Vow, Zero Latency |
| Farm | Sunny Paws Ranch |

New names should follow the same feel: plausible, slightly dramatic, two or three words.

## 2. Character voices

Each character has a **typing signature** (mechanics you can check), a **personality**, what they **reward** and what they **punish**. Match all four.

### Nyx — e-kitten, @nyxx.exe, 22

- **Typing:** almost entirely lowercase, including "i". Short fragments separated by periods. Leading "..." for hesitation. Abbreviations: u, ur, idc, ngl, pls, tmrw, lol, lmao, ok. Keeps apostrophes (don't, i'm, you're). Caps only in a panic ("delete it. DELETE IT.").
- **Personality:** deadpan, deflects everything, pretends not to care, and gives herself away. Soft moments are tiny and immediately undercut.
- **Rewards:** being unbothered, low-key presence, saying little, quiet acknowledgement ("noted."), keeping things chill.
- **Punishes:** fan energy, over-enthusiasm, big feelings speeches, making her soft moment "a thing", screenshotting, being too available.
- **Examples:**
  - "oh. new guy. ...hi i guess."
  - "please lower your volume. emotionally."
  - "big talk for someone with a default banner."
  - "ok that was smooth. i hate it."
  - "this is why i don't say things."
  - "ur breathing is weirdly calming. that's all."
- **Never:** "!!", emoticons, capitalized sentences, sincere declarations without a deflection attached.

### Goldie — puppy girl, @goldiewoof, 21

- **Typing:** mostly lowercase with ALL-CAPS bursts when excited. "!!", "!!!", "??". Stretched words (HIIII). Abbreviations: u, ur, rn, ngl. Emoticons like >_< and :( . Roleplay actions in asterisks (*happy tail wagging noises*). Parenthetical asides ("(lie if they're bad)"). Sends many messages at once.
- **Personality:** golden-retriever energy, sincere, generous, easily hurt but always polite about it. Dog imagery: tails, wagging, puppy, paws.
- **Rewards:** matching her enthusiasm, sincere care, standing up for her, making plans with her.
- **Punishes:** one-word replies ("k", "lol ok"), dismissiveness, calling her dramatic, ignoring her feelings.
- **When hurt:** goes short, polite and self-blaming: "oh. ok. sorry for the 17 messages lol". Even when she's lied to, she sounds more hurt than furious: "...i thought u were different".
- **Examples:**
  - "HIIII {name}!!! welcome to the server!!!"
  - "i'm literally kicking my feet rn"
  - "oh... ok! that's ok! ...k is a whole word right? >_<"
  - "ok my tail is wagging so hard it's a hazard"
  - "i'm sending a voice memo. it's 9 minutes. sorry"
  - "ur my favorite person to lose with"
- **Never:** cold sarcasm, proper sentence case for whole messages, cruelty.

### Rhea — bunny girl, @rhea.hops, 24

- **Typing:** proper sentence case, full stops, apostrophes. Short declarative sentences. Questions are challenges, not requests. **No "!!" and no emoticons.** A leading "..." marks when she softens ("...Fine. 8pm."). Rare caps for shock only ("You did WHAT.").
- **Personality:** top-ranked, in control, dry, competitive. Insults are crisp one-liners, often gaming metaphors (lag, ranked, carry, duo). She wants to be led, not worshipped.
- **Rewards:** taking the lead, owning mistakes, naming a time and place, confidence without begging, backing people up in drama.
- **Punishes:** begging, "whatever you want", fan worship, wishy-washy answers ("maybe", "we'll see"), insecurity.
- **Examples:**
  - "Prove you're not another guy who folds the second I look at him."
  - "And there it is. Folded in one sentence."
  - "Insecurity isn't cute. It's lag."
  - "Not bad. You're growing on me. Like mold."
  - "That's not convincing. That's a weather report."
  - "...Okay. 8pm."
- **Never:** "!!", lowercase-only messages, gushing, asking permission.

A rival's affection number is "his odds" with his crush. The player lowers it by confronting or outshining him, and raises it by deferring to him or liking his posts.

### The narrator

- Second person, present tense: "You join…", "She mutes her mic."
- One or two short sentences. Deadpan. Specific, concrete, often numeric details: "Seventeen messages in eleven seconds." "Call length: 6h 41m."
- Show feelings through platform actions, never by naming emotions:
  - "Nyx reacts to your message with a skull."
  - "His status changes to “do not disturb.”"
  - "For the first time all week, she logs off without having the last word."
- Rendered in italics; never written with a speaker.

## 3. Punctuation and formatting rules

- Curly quotes inside text: “like this”. Primes for height: 5′7″.
- En dash only for scores and ranges (3–1, 13–2). **Avoid em dashes** in dialogue and narration.
- Player actions in choices use asterisks: "*mutes the group chat*".
- Sunday-style direct messages use: Message Nyx: “hey. wanna hang out. or not. whatever.”
- Dialogue lines are short. Aim for under ~120 characters; most are under 60. Narration can run a little longer.
- Use `{name}` style dynamic text (functions of state) only when the line genuinely depends on state, like the player's gamertag or what they did earlier in the week.

### Slang

Stick to the vocabulary already in the game: lol, lmao, ngl, idc, rn, tmrw, pls, u, ur, ok, k, bro, bestie, cringe, mid, iconic, legend, unbothered, simp, gg, gm, carry, int, cracked, clutch, duo, queue, ranked, pfp, vc, dms, subtweet, close friends list.

Do not introduce trendy slang that dates quickly unless the project owner decides to add it to this list.

## 4. How choices are designed

### The three archetypes

Most scenes offer three choices, each a different stance. Each love interest rewards a different one:

| Archetype | Sounds like | Nyx | Goldie | Rhea |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Detached / chill** | lowercase, short: "sup.", "noted.", "she can think what she wants." | +2 | −1 to −2 | 0 to −1 |
| **Eager / over-the-top** | caps, "!!", "OMG", "literally", "I'll do anything" | −2 | +1 to +2 | −1 to −2 |
| **Confident / leading** | sentence case, commands, times and plans: "8pm. Don't be late." | 0 to −1 | 0 to +1 | +1 to +2 |

Two other stances recur:

- **Mature mediator** (calling out bad behavior, standing up for someone): usually Rhea +2, Goldie +1, Nyx −1.
- **Honest** (owning what happened): wins confrontations and side-character tests (Theo, Jade). Lying is the worst option (−3).

### Rules

- **No free lunch in group scenes.** A choice that pleases one girl should cost something with another.
- **Vary the position** of the best option. It must not always be first or last.
- **Every option is a real personality,** not a strawman. A player should be tempted by each one.
- **Single-girl tiered scenes** (low / mid / high): typically one +2, one around 0 or +1, one −2.
  - Low tier: she's guarded; the test is whether you match her style.
  - Mid tier: she opens up a little; the trap is overreacting to it.
  - High tier: she's vulnerable; the trap is coldness or turning it into a big deal.
- **Side-character scenes** move that character ±1 or ±2 and sometimes nudge a girl ±1.
- **Rival scenes:** standing up to the rival lowers his odds (−1 or −2); deferring raises them.

### Reactions

- Each choice gets a pool of 2–3 alternative reactions, so replays feel different. All alternatives must carry the same meaning and score.
- Group-scene reactions are short line sequences, usually two lines from different characters.
- An optional narration line can follow a reaction to show a consequence.

## 5. Numbers and balance

Keep new content on the existing scale so thresholds stay meaningful.

| Thing | Value |
|---|---|
| Typical choice delta | ±1 or ±2 |
| Betrayal-level delta (switching matching pfps, lying in a confrontation) | −3 |
| Character creation effects | ±1 or ±2 per trait |
| Starting affection: supporting cast / rivals | 0 / 2 |
| Scene tiers | low < 3, mid 3–6, high ≥ 7 |
| Matching pfp unlock / sleep call unlock | 6 / 8 |
| Ending | left ≤ 3, friends 4–8, good ≥ 9 |

- Never give more than +2 to one character from a single choice.
- A day has three scenes. If you add days or scenes, check that a focused playthrough still reaches 9+ with one girl and doesn't trivially max out everyone.
- New mechanics with bigger swings need a note in `docs/PORT_NOTES.md` or a design note explaining why.

## 6. Chirp posts, endings and epilogues

### Posts

- One post per love interest per day, plus two from the supporting cast or rivals.
- A love interest's post can be a `[warm, cold]` pair: warm if she likes you (affection ≥ 3), cold if not. Both versions should hint at that day's events without naming the player.
  - Warm: "baked cookies for someone special!! (shaped like a gamertag) (u know who u are)"
  - Cold: "baked cookies. ate them all myself. i'm fine!!"
- Like replies: 1–5 words, in voice ("HE LIKED IT!!!", "Obviously.", "...ty i guess").

### Endings

- **Title:** 2–4 words, platform- or gaming-flavored: "Player 2 has joined", "Left on read", "Crowned", "Blocked", "Duo partner".
- **Body:** 3–5 sentences, narrator voice, can quote her once. End on a platform detail as the punchline (a bio change, a stream title, a profile banner, a ranked screenshot).
- Each love interest has good, friends and left endings.

### Epilogues ("Meanwhile")

- One sentence per non-picked girl, at high, mid and low tiers, told through a platform action: "Goldie quietly unfollows you. Somehow that hurts more than yelling."

## 7. Adding a new love interest

Fill this in before writing any lines:

- **Archetype:** an animal-themed e-girl type with matching ears for the avatar (existing: e-kitten, puppy girl, bunny girl).
- **Handle, age (21+), one-line bio.**
- **Typing signature:** at least three mechanical rules that make her instantly distinguishable from Nyx, Goldie and Rhea in a single line.
- **Rewards / punishes:** which archetype she favors, so she doesn't overlap exactly with an existing girl.
- **Best friend** (supporting cast) and **rival** (with his own typing signature).
- **Full content set:** portrait, avatar theme, evening lines (low/mid/high for queue and watch), milestone lines, a post for every day, like replies, confrontation, three endings, three epilogues, and reactions in every game and watch option.

## 8. Self-check before you finish

- [ ] Could you identify the speaker of every line with the name hidden?
- [ ] Nyx lines are lowercase, Rhea lines have no "!!", Goldie lines have energy.
- [ ] Narration is second person, present tense, deadpan, shown through platform actions.
- [ ] No real brands, games, streamers or people.
- [ ] No em dashes in dialogue or narration; curly quotes inside text.
- [ ] Each choice is a different archetype, and the best option isn't always in the same position.
- [ ] Deltas are within ±2 (or −3 for a betrayal).
- [ ] Every reaction pool has 2–3 alternatives with the same meaning.
- [ ] Heat level stays at flirtation; all characters adults.
- [ ] No existing lines were changed.

## 9. Automated checks (warnings, not failures)

The content validator should warn on:

- Rhea lines containing "!!" or emoticons.
- Nyx lines where most letters are uppercase, or that contain "!!".
- Dialogue lines over 140 characters.
- Em dashes in dialogue or narration.
- Choice deltas outside −3 to +2.
- A reaction pool with only one alternative.

Characters break pattern on purpose sometimes (Nyx's "DELETE IT."), so these are warnings for a human to review.
